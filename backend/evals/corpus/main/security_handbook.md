# Security Handbook

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every employee account.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption with BitLocker. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour.

## Incidents
Report any security incident to the security team at security@kestrel.example within 24 hours. Phishing emails are forwarded to phishing@kestrel.example and then deleted.
