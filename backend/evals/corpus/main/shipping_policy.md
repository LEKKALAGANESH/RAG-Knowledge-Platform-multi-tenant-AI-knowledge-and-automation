# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping delivers within 2 business days and costs 15 EUR per parcel.

Shipping is free for orders above 100 EUR. We ship to all EU countries, the United Kingdom and Norway. We do not currently ship to Switzerland.

Every parcel gets a tracking number that is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Parcels heavier than 30 kg are sent by freight and need a delivery appointment.
