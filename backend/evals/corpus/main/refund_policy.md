# Refund Policy

Kestrel Logistics customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refund requests are submitted through the customer portal under Billing > Refunds.

## After 30 days
Between day 31 and day 90 we offer store credit instead of a cash refund. After 90 days no refund or credit is possible.

## Exceptions
Digital goods such as route-optimisation licences are non-refundable once activated. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
