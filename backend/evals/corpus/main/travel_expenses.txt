Travel and Expense Guidelines

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night in most cities and up to 250 EUR per night in London, Paris and Zurich.
The daily meal allowance (per diem) is 45 EUR.
Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy with manager approval.
Expense claims, including all receipts, must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the trip.
Taxis are reimbursed only when public transport is not available or after 22:00.
